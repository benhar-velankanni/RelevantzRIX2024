Algorithm:

1. Start Program.
2. Open the online bus booking portal.
3. Enter the departure city.
4. Enter the destination city.
5. Select the travel date.
6. Search for available buses.
7. Display the list of available buses.
8. Select the desired bus.
    IF bus not available, then go to step 8.
9. Select the seat(s).
    IF seats not available, then go to step 9.
10. Enter passenger details (name, age, contact information).
11. Proceed to payment.
12. Enter payment details (credit card, debit card, net banking, etc.).
13. Confirm payment.
14. Generate the ticket.
15. Send ticket details to the passenger via email/SMS.
16. Stop Program.
