Algorithm:

1. Start Program.
2. Get the value of three numbers and store it in variables (a), (b) and (c).
3. Compare (a) and (b):
    IF (a > b), go to step 4.
    ELSE go to step 5.
4. Compare (a) and (c):
    IF (a > c), then (a) is the largest, go to step 6.
    ELSE go to step 5.
5. Compare (b) and (c):
    IF (b > c), then (b) is the largest, go to step 6.
    ELSE (c) is the largest, go to step 6.
6. Print the largest number.
7. Stop Program.
