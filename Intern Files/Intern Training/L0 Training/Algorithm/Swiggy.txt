Algorithm:

1. Start Program.
2. Open the Swiggy app.
3. Log in to your account (if not already logged in).
4. Search for the desired restaurant or type of cuisine.
5. Browse the menu.
6. Select the favorite food item.
7. Add the selected item to the cart.
8. Review the cart and make any necessary modifications.
9. Proceed to checkout
10. Enter delivery address or select a saved address.
11. Choose the payment method.
12. Complete the payment process.
13. Confirm the order.
14. Wait for order confirmation and estimated delivery time.
15. End Program.
