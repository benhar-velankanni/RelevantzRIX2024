Anime
Anime is a style of animation that originated in Japan and has become popular worldwide. It encompasses a wide range of genres and themes, appealing to audiences of all ages. The art style is often characterized by vibrant colors, exaggerated facial expressions, and fantastical settings, although it can vary widely depending on the creator's vision.

Manga
Manga refers to a style of graphic novels or comics that originated in Japan. These works often feature intricate artwork and storytelling and are created in a variety of genres, including action, romance, fantasy, horror, and more. Manga is typically serialized in magazines and later collected into volumes called tankobon.

Manhwa
Manhwa refers to comics and graphic novels originating from South Korea. Like Japanese manga, manhwa spans a wide range of genres, from romance and fantasy to action and drama. It is traditionally read from left to right, following the Korean writing system's orientation, which differs from the right-to-left format of Japanese manga.
